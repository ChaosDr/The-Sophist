The Sophist

A classroom that learns who gets to speak

You are a student in a secondary-school philosophy class. Your teacher says he wants rigorous thinking. Some students seem to have more room to think out loud than others.

Over a semester, you will notice small moments, witness a presentation, and decide what you might realistically do. You do not need to know whether a philosophical argument is correct to notice how it is treated.

Two changing indicators reflect one possible imagined classroom: Room to speak and Shared support. They are storytelling devices, not measurements of real people. Your choices matter, but you cannot control the teacher or guarantee anyone's safety.
